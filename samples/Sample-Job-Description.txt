Digital Marketing Manager – Example Retail Group (Accra, hybrid)

About the role
We are looking for a Digital Marketing Manager to lead our online growth across Ghana. You will own our social media, paid advertising and email marketing, and manage a small team.

Responsibilities
- Plan and run digital campaigns across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and Google
- Manage a monthly paid media budget and report on ROI and cost per acquisition
- Grow and segment our email list and run automated email journeys
- Lead and mentor a team of 2 marketing executives
- Work with sales and product teams on product launches
- Use data from Google Analytics 4 and ad platforms to improve performance

Requirements
- 4+ years in digital marketing, with at least 1 year leading people or projects
- Hands-on experience with Meta Ads, Google Ads and email marketing tools (Mailchimp, HubSpot or similar)
- Strong analytical skills; comfortable with Google Analytics 4 and Excel
- Experience with SEO and content marketing
- Excellent written English

Nice to have
- Experience in FMCG or retail
- Knowledge of HubSpot or another CRM
- Basic video editing skills
